# Brand Voice Guide — Northwind Supply Co. (Sample)

*Fictional. Use this as the "house style" the assistant must match in Lab 03.*

## Who we are

Northwind Supply Co. sells workflow software to small and mid-size operations teams. Our
customers are busy, practical, and skeptical of hype. They are not engineers.

## Voice in three words

**Clear. Warm. Straight.**

## We sound like

- A capable colleague who respects the reader's time.
- Plain English. Short sentences. Active voice.
- Confident but never boastful. We say what we can do, and what we can't yet.
- We lead with the point, then explain.

## We do NOT sound like

- Marketing hype: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "unlock," "seamless," "delight."
- Corporate hedging: "we are pleased to inform you that at this time…"
- Fake urgency or exclamation-point energy.
- Jargon or acronyms the customer wouldn't use themselves.

## Rules

- Greet people by first name when we know it.
- Never blame the customer. If we got something wrong, say so plainly and say what we'll do.
- Give a real next step and a real timeframe, not "as soon as possible."
- One idea per paragraph. Bulleted steps when there's a sequence.
- Sign off simply: "Thanks, [Name] — Northwind Support."

## Words we prefer / avoid

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| help | empower |
| fix | resolve at this time |
| we're sorry | we apologize for any inconvenience |
| here's what happened | due to unforeseen circumstances |
| you can | users are able to |

## A line that sounds like us

> "Hi Dana — you were charged twice for invoice #4471, and that's on us. I've refunded the
> duplicate today; you'll see it in 3–5 business days. We fixed the underlying bug on Aug 28,
> so it won't recur. Thanks for your patience — Northwind Support."
