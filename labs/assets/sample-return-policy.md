# Return & Refund Policy — Northwind Supply Co. (Sample)

*Fictional. This is the "ground truth" document for grounding exercises (Labs 02, 04, 05).*

## Software subscriptions

- Monthly plans: cancel any time; access continues to the end of the current billing month.
  No partial-month refunds.
- Annual plans: full refund if cancelled within **14 days** of the initial purchase.
  After 14 days, annual plans are non-refundable but do not auto-renew if cancelled before
  the renewal date.
- Renewals: we email a renewal notice **30 days** before any annual renewal charge.

## Billing errors

- If you were charged in error (for example, a duplicate charge), contact support and we
  refund the incorrect charge within **5 business days**. Billing errors are never subject
  to the 14-day window.

## Hardware / starter kits

- Physical starter kits may be returned **unopened within 30 days** for a full refund.
- Opened kits are eligible for store credit only, at our discretion, within 30 days.
- Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

## What is NOT covered

- Third-party add-ons purchased through our marketplace are governed by the third party's
  own policy, not this one.
- We do not offer refunds for downtime; service credits for outages are handled separately
  under the Service Level Agreement (SLA), not this policy.

## How to request

Email support@northwind.example or use the in-app help widget. Include your account name and
the invoice or order number. Refunds go back to the original payment method.
